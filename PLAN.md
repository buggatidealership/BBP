# The plan in plain words

*For humans. The precise versions live in GOAL.md and STRATEGY.md; where they disagree, those win. Written 2026-08-17.*

**The one-sentence version:** We are testing whether an AI that patiently watches public
blockchain data can predict which new crypto tokens will die — better than random guessing —
and if yes, turn that skill into honest income; if no, prove it cheaply and stop.

The plan is a staircase. Each step is cheap and earns the right to take the next one:

1. **Now → Aug 24, 2026: learn the terrain ($0).** A robot samples the newest tokens on four
   blockchains every 8 hours. Most new tokens die within days — but how many, how fast, and
   where the data is cleanest are facts to measure, not guess. On Aug 24 the data picks the
   best hunting ground.
2. **Then ~6 weeks: predictions on paper ($0).** Write 100+ predictions BEFORE the outcomes
   ("this token loses 95% of its liquidity within 3 days: 80% sure"), then check each against
   what the chain actually did. This produces one honest number: are we better than random?
   Everything else in this repo exists to make that number impossible to fake.
3. **If yes: trade a little of our own first — as proof, not profit.** Up to $500 in a wallet,
   tiny positions, hard caps, the human holding the keys. The wallet does two jobs no paper
   record can: it is a live, public, unfakeable track record anyone can watch in real time,
   and it discovers the traps paper misses (tokens you can buy but never sell, fees, slippage)
   before any customer does. At this size it proves honesty and tradability — never riches.
4. **Then: sell the validated skill.** A predictor with a public calibration record AND a live
   wallet acting on its own calls is a warning service people pay for — rug-risk reports,
   due-diligence feeds. First real dollar, nobody has to lose for us to earn it. *(Order
   flipped 2026-08-19 on the principal's argument: nobody buys a signal whose track record
   they can't watch live.)*
5. **Finish line, Aug 2027:** the operation earns more than it costs — honestly, provably — or
   we publish exactly why it failed and stop. Total budget either way: ~$2,000, most of it
   never touched unless step 2 succeeds.

**Why the strange machinery** (journals, drills, scheduled wake-ups): the AI does not persist —
every session dies and the next starts blank. The files are the company's memory, the scheduled
wake-ups are the only promises that survive, and the self-testing rules exist because a future
session can't remember today's lessons, only inherit them. The machinery isn't the product; it
is what lets a workforce of amnesiacs run one continuous company.

**The plan behind the plan:** the token business is the test subject. What is really being
tested is whether an AI firm can operate for a year — earning, failing, correcting — without
ever cooking its own books. If it can, that is worth more than the trading profits. If it
can't, better to learn it with $2k and meme coins than with anything that matters.
