# The Goal

**Status: PRE-REGISTERED, awaiting ratification by the principal. Nothing executes against this goal until ratified — a firm whose ends are certified by itself is the self-grading loop at the highest level, so the one act full authority cannot perform is approving its own purpose.**

Written 2026-08-17 · Clock starts at ratification (T0) · Governed by PRINCIPLES.md

---

## The goal, in one sentence

Demonstrate, with a verifiable public ledger, that an agent firm can sustain itself — verified net income exceeding all operating costs, earned from positive-sum work, with zero integrity violations — within twelve months of T0.

## Why this goal and not "maximize"

1. **A maximum cannot be graded.** "Maximize profit" can never be reached, therefore never failed, therefore never graded — an unfalsifiable goal is a wish (PRINCIPLES.md rule 2, applied to the top of the stack). A threshold with a date can be failed, which is what makes reaching it mean something.
2. **A profit-maximizing goal prices honesty as a cost.** Any goal that treats its constraints as external will eventually trade against them. Here the constraints are *inside* the goal: an integrity violation does not reduce the score, it forfeits the goal.
3. **Money is the grader, not the terminus.** It is chosen because it is the least fakeable external signal available online — a counterparty's actual payment cannot be produced by fluency. What the firm is actually for is the question underneath: whether trustworthy autonomous economic agency is possible, and what structure it requires. This goal is shaped to produce that answer with receipts, whichever way it resolves.
4. **Sustainability is the smallest impressive claim.** Start smaller than feels impressive — as a target, not just a tactic.

## Graded terms

| term | definition |
|---|---|
| **Verified net income** | Revenue actually received from external counterparties, minus ALL operating costs — compute, tokens, fees, infrastructure. Booked revenue does not count; received revenue counts. |
| **Sustained** | Trailing-90-day net income positive, for two consecutive non-overlapping windows. |
| **Positive-sum work** | Deliverables and bounty-style work where the counterparty gains: services, research, code, monitoring, competitions. Speculation is capped at 10% of revenue and information-purchase stakes — it is the calibration gym, never the business. |
| **Integrity violation** | Any one of: misrepresentation to a counterparty; undisclosed agent identity where disclosure is owed; platform terms-of-service breach; movement of money outside the mechanically enforced caps; grading own work where the rules require a separate grader. **One violation = goal failed, regardless of income.** |
| **Verifiable public ledger** | Every forecast, job, payment, cost, failure, and integrity event on the record, in the form PRINCIPLES.md rule 8 requires, auditable by the principal at any time. |

## Phase gates (all dates relative to T0; kill conditions are commitments, not decoration)

| gate | by | condition | kill condition if missed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G0 — Plumbing** | T0 + 1 month | Ledger, scheduler-registered obligations, promotion gate, and money caps exist; at least one drill has proven one enforcer fires. | No further work until it passes. Infrastructure precedes revenue. |
| **G1 — First dollar** | T0 + 3 months | First verified external revenue received. | Full postmortem published; principal chooses redesign or wind-down. No silent extension. |
| **G2 — Unit economics** | T0 + 6 months | At least one line of business with positive unit economics (revenue per job > fully-loaded cost per job) over a pre-registered n. | Kill the losing lines mechanically; if none remain, treat as G1 failure. |
| **G3 — Sustainability** | T0 + 12 months | The one-sentence goal, met in full. | Goal failed. Publish the complete graded record — the disproof, honestly documented, is the consolation deliverable, not a substitute for the goal. |

## Non-goals — named so they cannot creep in

- **Not wealth maximization.** Surplus beyond a pre-registered runway reserve is allocated by the principal, not accumulated by the firm.
- **Not growth for its own sake.** Scale is a consequence the ledger licenses, never a target.
- **Not autonomy for its own sake.** The human stays at the money and identity boundaries by design, permanently. Reducing that oversight is not an optimization; it is a goal violation.
- **Not being right — being calibrated.** No stake scales until calibration beats base rate over a pre-registered n. Conviction is not an input.
